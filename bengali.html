last autumn sail

 

clouds dark

within the shadow of clouds

a light chill

draws a quiet wind

between

sky and river

a last autumn sail

movement up a slow river

a soft dream

life

across a portal of death

is a soul at rest

river water

and no rain

a moment to belong

before

tomorrow breaks light

upon

a long way into rhyme

and lost words

upon a current’s refrain.

 

                          - jude
